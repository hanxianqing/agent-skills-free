## What is in here

### `shorts-editing/` — Shorts Editing Skill

Turn product images + a voiceover into a vertical Shorts video that does **not** look like a static slideshow. This is the exact v2 pipeline we used for our Shorts in November 2026.

- **SKILL.md** — the full recipe: hook card, burned-in subtitles, crossfades, rotating camera moves, progress bar, adaptive title size, the ffmpeg probe → cut → verify flow, the `zoompan d=1` pitfall that cost us a day, five visual experiments with verdicts, and YouTube publishing rules (verified November 2026).
- **qa-checklist.md** — run before every upload. One failure = do not upload.
- **shot-list-template.md** — copy per video, fill in your product, hook, captions and subtitles.

Requirements: `ffmpeg` + `ffprobe`. No other dependencies.
